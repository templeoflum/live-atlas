# Workspace families

Design pass · September 22, 2026

## Structure

Working tabs: Workspace, Clips, Devices. Overview and Live’s History remain separate.
Sets is not a territory: Live Sets are documents under Workspace → Files.

Workspace uses a fixed family selector above a filtered place list, with the
existing action bar in the card. Changing family filters the list without
changing the open card. Choosing a place opens its actions. Only the lower list
scrolls; the family controls do not expand or move. Narrow screens have paired
Family and Place selectors. Mac/Windows keys continue to apply to the card.

| Family | Places |
| --- | --- |
| Navigation | Views, Control bar, Editing, Shortcuts |
| Browser | Search & load, Filters & collections, Library & folders, Packs & connections |
| Session | Clip grid, Scenes, Record a performance |
| Arrangement | Timeline, Clips on the timeline, Time & tracks, Automation, Envelope editing, Video |
| Mixer | Tracks, Levels & pan, Returns & Main, Solo & cue, Timing & load, Bounce to audio |
| Routing | Audio connections, MIDI connections, Between tracks, Shared instruments, Synchronization |
| Files | Live Sets, Projects, Samples & dependencies, Reusable material, Folders & versions, Export, Audio quality |
| Settings & controllers | Settings, Controllers |

Clips is for editing the material inside a clip. Devices is for instruments and
processors. Working with several clips, tracks and their signal paths belongs
in the corresponding Workspace place. Arrangement-specific zooming and track
folding live with the timeline and tracks, not only in general navigation.

## Content and next pass

Existing authored guides are reused in action cards with their source links.
Children are visible in the card, not another disclosure. The current authored
material from manual chapters 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 17, 18, 20 and 25 is reachable in
Workspace. This does not mean every parameter is fully converted.

Video, synchronization, audio-fact-sheet subsections and broader references such
as Push and accessibility still include source-only material. They need the
same text-to-action treatment before declaring the manual complete.

No image files are created, edited or downloaded in this pass. Existing captures
stay in place. Once text is ready, prefer appropriate official-manual figures;
custom images and short action loops are a later pass. This is a design-stage
site, so historical route compatibility is not a design requirement.
